How a commission comes together

  1. 01

    Inquiry & consultation

    You reach out through the form below with references, size, and budget in mind. I reply within 2–3 days with availability and a starting quote.

  2. 02

    Concept & deposit

    Once we agree on scope, I send a fixed quote and invoice. A 50% deposit secures your place in the queue.

  3. 03

    Sketch approval

    You'll receive a rough composition sketch. This is the stage for structural changes — pose, layout, palette direction.

  4. 04

    The work itself

    I paint or render in stages, sharing progress photos along the way for digital and traditional pieces alike.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Digital files arrive print-ready; original paintings are carefully packed and shipped, or available for local pickup.

Guidelines & policy

Terms of service

Please review before placing a commission request.

Payment & deposits

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to begin work and secures your place in the queue. The remaining balance is due before digital files are sent or a painting ships.

Revisions

One round of structural revisions is included at the sketch stage. Changes requested after painting or rendering has begun may incur an additional fee.

Timeline

Turnaround is typically 2–6 weeks depending on medium and current queue length. Rush requests are considered case by case for an added fee.

Usage & rights

Commissioned work is for your personal, non-commercial use unless a commercial license is agreed in writing beforehand. I retain the right to display the piece in my portfolio and on social media.

What I won't draw

I'm happy to discuss most subjects, but I decline hate symbols, explicit content, and extremely dense scenes without a separate complexity quote. When in doubt, ask.

Cancellations

You may cancel before the sketch stage for a refund of any amount paid beyond the deposit. Once painting or final rendering has begun, payments are non-refundable.

Note: I may ask you to fill out a brief form acknowledging these terms before starting work.
